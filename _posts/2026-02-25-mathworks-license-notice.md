The statewide academic license agreement for MathWorks products (such as MATLAB) at universities in Baden-Württemberg is valid until **March 31, 2026**. **On NEMO2 specifically, there will be no general MathWorks license available after this date.** Please note that universities and HPC clusters in Baden-Württemberg may handle this differently — check with your local IT or HPC support for details.

## Recommended Alternatives

We encourage users to migrate to open-source alternatives, for example:

- [GNU Octave](https://octave.org/) – largely MATLAB-compatible scripting language
- [Julia](https://julialang.org/) – high-performance language for numerical and scientific computing
- [Python](https://www.python.org/) with libraries such as NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib
- [R](https://www.r-project.org/) – statistical computing and graphics

Not all of these are available on NEMO2 yet, but we can provide them on request. Please contact the helpdesk (see below).

## Using Your Own MathWorks License

If you have access to MATLAB through your own or your institution's license, the following options may be available:

- Pre-compile MATLAB code (MCR/MATLAB Runtime) using your own license and run the compiled executables on NEMO2 without requiring a license at runtime.
- Working groups with their own MathWorks license may be able to use MATLAB on NEMO2 under suitable conditions. Please contact us to discuss how your license can be configured appropriately.

## Questions or Requests?

If you would like to use MATLAB on NEMO2 with your own license or need help migrating to an alternative, please submit a ticket to the central helpdesk:
[https://bw-support.scc.kit.edu](https://bw-support.scc.kit.edu)
